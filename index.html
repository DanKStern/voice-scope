Voice Scope

A kid-friendly way to see your voice as a picture — a companion tool for the Technology for Young Learners curriculum. Works in any modern browser with a microphone, nothing to install.

Press Start to wake up the microphone.
Your voice over time — how loud
Zoomed way in — the wiggles

🔒 Nothing is uploaded or saved — your voice never leaves this computer. Recording only happens when you press Record, and only lasts 4 seconds.

Record something, then switch the animals on and off to hear each part of your voice.

Grown-up settings
Talk normally and slide this until your voice reaches the “talking” line.
Bands: Bear < 300 Hz · Person 300–1000 Hz · Bird 1000–3000 Hz · Snake > 3000 Hz (4th-order crossover filters). Add ?test to the address to use a built-in test sound instead of the microphone.
